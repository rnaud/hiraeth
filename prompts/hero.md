# The traveller (the hero)

Character exploration sheet for a solitary desert wanderer in a surreal science fiction adventure. Oversized lavender flight suit, salmon gloves, glass bubble helmet, blue headphones, weathered radio backpack with a long antenna, dangling cables, utility pouches, and a tiny turquoise scout drone emerging from the pack. Six distinct variations exploring silhouette, helmet shape, backpack design, fabric layers, and desert survival equipment. Full body, readable silhouettes, restrained detail, warm cream background. Franco-Belgian science fiction comic, ligne claire ink drawing, thin precise contours, flat pastel colors, subtle paper texture, sparse crosshatching, whimsical industrial design, quiet mystery. Preserve the handmade illustrated feeling of the reference. No text, no photorealism, no heavy black shadows. Moebius style.
