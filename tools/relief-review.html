Painted relief: before and after

Beside each mark's ink line, a thin lighter line on the side facing the sun and a thin darker one on the side away from it, oriented by the sun's way across the surface (so they agree with the cast shadows). No normal maps: still flat colour, the darker line a tone of the surface's own colour, never ink. It is off in the game until you pick it. To try it live, add ?relief=1 to a world's address, or use the dev panel's Beauty › “painted relief (review)”.

Drag across a picture to compare (left: off, right: on). The close-ups are the dashed box at 3×, pixel for pixel. Pictures and numbers come from node tools/relief-shots.mjs: a muted headless Chrome on the real GPU, 1280 × 720, noon, clear, enemies off, the HUD hidden, a pinned camera; off and on are the same load of the same build with only the switch flipped.
