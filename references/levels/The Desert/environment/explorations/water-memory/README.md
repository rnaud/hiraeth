# The memory of water

The Desert, direction C: the water that has not risen left its marks: dry riverbeds with stranded boats, salt
pans with mirages of the old lake, a line of qanat wells to listen at, a hamlet in a dry cistern; when the tree
drinks, the salt pan floods. Prompts `de-water-memory-establishing` and `de-water-memory-ground` in
docs/design/level-explorations-prompts.md; the author's picks land here.
