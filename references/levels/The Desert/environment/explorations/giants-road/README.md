# The giants' road

The Desert, direction A: the giants fell one by one along their road from the swamp of lights; a line of their
bones crosses the dunes (a hand like a ruin, an arm bridging a dry wash, a spine to climb), each with a pilgrims'
shrine and a verse. Prompts `de-giants-road-establishing` and `de-giants-road-ground` in
docs/design/level-explorations-prompts.md; the author's picks land here.
