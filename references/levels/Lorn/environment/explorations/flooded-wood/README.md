# The flooded wood

Lorn, direction C. The swamp's water runs into the Deep Wood: the crossing does not end at a shore but in a drowned forest. Trunks rise out of the open water, closer and closer, the roof closes overhead, the light goes from twilight blue to rose, and the skiff threads between them under root arches into flooded clearings where shafts of light fall on islands of moss and egg heaps.
