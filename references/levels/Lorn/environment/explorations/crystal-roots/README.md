# The roots of the crystal

Lorn, direction A. The Great Crystal is the tip of something huge. Its roots run under the swamp: veins of violet and teal crystal under the water, across the whole basin, breaking the surface as the groves. In the rain the crystal sings and the veins light under the water, a pattern converging on the Crystal like the veins of a leaf.
