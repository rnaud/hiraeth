# The silent canyons

Vael, direction B. The dry canyons are the riders' old roads, sunk below the plain: narrow, winding, walled in red rock, with carved niches, stone ears on the walls and steps cut down to them. In a silent world sound carries in a canyon: the riders spoke here only in whispers, and a word said aloud runs ahead of you round the bends.
