# Where the stones fall up

Vael, direction A. Toward the cliff the plain is coming apart: first cracks, then slabs tilted out of the ground, then slabs a metre up, then whole tables of plain hanging over the cloud with the needles still standing on them, roots of rock trailing below. The two halves of Vael become one slope from the ground to the sky.
