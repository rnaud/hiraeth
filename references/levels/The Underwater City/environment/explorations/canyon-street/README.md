# The canyon street

The Underwater City, direction C. From the references: the city does not stop at the drop, it goes down it. A street of pods stacked on every storey clings to the canyon wall over the deep, a stair of terraces and arched bridges going down, lit cafés on ledges, lamps getting fewer as it goes, until the Whale Gallery at the bottom by the dark water.
