# The city that sings

The Underwater City, direction B. The city was built as an instrument for the whales: great brass horns open from the domes into the sea, organ pipes stand in the halls, the Breathing Tower is a pipe of rising bubbles, and the glass itself is tuned. Since the wrong note the city hums out of tune, and you can see it: standing ripples in the domes' pools, bubbles rising out of time, a shiver in the glass.
