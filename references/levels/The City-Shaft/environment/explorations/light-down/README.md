# The light that goes down

The City-Shaft, direction C: a chain of great mirrors throws the sun down the shaft from terrace to terrace; the
beam crawls round the wall through the day, and turning the broken mirrors back lights the depths. Prompts
`cs-light-down-establishing` and `cs-light-down-ground` in docs/design/level-explorations-prompts.md; the
author's picks land here.
