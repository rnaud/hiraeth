# The old market below

The Signal Market, direction C. The avenue is a deck over the old market: a sunken street a storey or two down, the market's first one, glimpsed through brass grilles in the paving and reached by stairs at the corners. Down there daylight falls through the gratings in stripes, the stalls are older and fuller (the dense heaps of the references), the oldest sign hangs over a crossing, and the first stall, where an answer was first sold, still opens.
