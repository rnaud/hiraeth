# The tooth calendar

The Buried Machine, direction C: the great wheel's teeth are the dome people's years, carved and painted; its rim
is a stair across the dunes, and sinkholes lead down to a hall of turning gears where a keeper lives in a hollow
tooth. Prompts `bm-tooth-calendar-establishing` and `bm-tooth-calendar-ground` in
docs/design/level-explorations-prompts.md; the author's picks land here.
