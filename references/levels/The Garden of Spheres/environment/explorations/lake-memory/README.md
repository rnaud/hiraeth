# What the lake remembers

The Garden of Spheres, direction C. The mirror lake does not reflect the garden as it is: it shows the garden as the spheres remember it, before they fell, with white towns that are gone, people on the shore, the spheres hanging in the sky like moons. Causeways of white stepping stones cross it just above the water.
