# Where the moons fell

The Garden of Spheres, direction A. The spheres are the Moon Foundry's first moons, fallen short, and the land should show the falls. Each great sphere lies at the end of the scar it made: a long furrow ploughed through the meadow, rings of thrown-up earth turned into terraces, a crater that filled to a lake, and one sphere cracked open on landing, a hollow room inside it.
