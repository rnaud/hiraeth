# The stacked quarters

The City Floating in Space, direction C. From the references: the city is not one storey of islands but a cliff of them. The Towers become a stack of small islands one above the other, houses built down their sides and up into the next, joined by stairs, double arches, ladders and bridges at many heights, so a quarter is a vertical town 100 m high.
