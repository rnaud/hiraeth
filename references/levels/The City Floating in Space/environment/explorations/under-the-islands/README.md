# Under the islands

The City Floating in Space, direction A. The islands have an underside, and the moorers live on it: catwalks and ladders hung under the decks among the machinery, pipes and cables, upside-down gardens hanging into the void, little workshops in the rock, lamps pointing down at the stars below.
