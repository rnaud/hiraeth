# The singing ridges

The Glass Dunes, direction B: the glassworkers have bored the crests into wind organs, chimes and flutes; the
valley is found by ear, the crests are climbed to tune them, and a sandstorm makes the whole basin sing. Prompts
`gd-singing-ridges-establishing` and `gd-singing-ridges-ground` in docs/design/level-explorations-prompts.md; the
author's picks land here.
