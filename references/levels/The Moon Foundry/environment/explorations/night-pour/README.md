# The night pour

The Moon Foundry, direction C. The Casting-House pours by itself at night, and the floor shows it: channels in the slabs, dry by day, fill with molten metal after dark, glowing runnels branching across the hangar to the moulds, the moulds cracking, the floor shaking, the foundry lit only by the pour.
