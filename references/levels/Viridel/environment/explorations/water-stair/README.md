# The water stair

Viridel, direction B. The gardeners grow on the slope as on a stair: Viridel rises from the pond in long terraces of white stone, each a bed of a different flower, fed by channels and little falls from the Greenhouse's hill, with sluices between them. The runnel and the tea terraces are the first steps of it.
