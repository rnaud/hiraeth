# The garden of fallen things

Viridel, direction A. Odile and Talo's ship is not the only thing the garden has kept: everything that ever fell on Viridel is still here, grown over. The meadow's hills are wrecks: an old round ship under a hill of flowers with a tree growing out of its hatch, a satellite's dish turned into a pond, a meteor split open with a garden in it, a builders' machine lying on its side as a terrace.
