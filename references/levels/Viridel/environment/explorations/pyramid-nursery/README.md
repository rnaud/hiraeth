# The pyramid nursery

Viridel, direction C. Oro grows pyramids, and Viridel is where they are grown: not a scatter, a nursery. Rows of white step pyramids at every age, from knee-high seedlings in beds, to houses, to the 90 m old one with trees on its terraces, the white androids tending them like gardeners, scaffolds of vines on the young ones.
