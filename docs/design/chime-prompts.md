# The chimes, floating: reference prompts

2026-10-09. The chimes (the currency, `src/chimes.js`, docs/systems/items.md "Chimes") grew back to the old brass
coin's size (a one 27.5 cm long, a five a 38 cm cluster) and now float, 40 cm of air under each, a soft patch of shade
on the ground below. The first reference (`references/Core Objects/Currency/Small Floating Crystal/`) was drawn for a
3 cm shard hovering over a palm; the author asked for a new design for the floating crystals. Plain text prompts for
the reference lab (`reference-lab.html`, `scripts/gen-reference.mjs --from docs/design/chime-prompts.md#<id>`).

Target folder: `references/Core Objects/Currency/Floating Chime/<id>/`

What every prompt keeps: a splinter of the singing mineral (it rings like struck glass when taken), translucent
cyan with a pale lavender seam inside and a soft light at its heart, broad weathered facets (a blunt shard, not a
gem or a rupee), floating upright and slightly tilted at knee height over the ground with its own small shadow
below, the small cloaked traveller with the round jade backpack for scale, the house style.
