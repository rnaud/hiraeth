# The fluid backpack, round and glowing: reference prompts

2026-10-10. Many of the reference lab's recent pictures (the enemy sheets' scale figure, the shops, the temples)
drew the traveller with **a round backpack with a green glow**, and the author likes it. These prompts explore a
backpack redesign in that direction: a round glass tank of living jade fluid, glowing, worn between the shoulder
blades. The current one (`references/Core Objects/Fluid Backpack - Wearable v2/`) is a flat rounded rectangle with a
hose to the glove; the hose was dropped (author, October 2026: "remove the cable from the tank to the glove").
Plain text prompts for the reference lab (`scripts/gen-reference.mjs --from docs/design/backpack-prompts.md#<id>`).

Target folder: `references/Core Objects/Round Backpack/<id>/`

What every prompt keeps: the traveller as in the game (a slim young adult, messy short black curly hair, coral
overshirt, tan neckerchief, cream trousers, small suede boots, a sword on his back is fine), the house style, a
plain cream background unless the prompt says otherwise, no hose or cable to the glove.
