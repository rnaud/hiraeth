# Alien-built temples: reference prompts

2026-10-14, issue #342: "I want some of the temples to feel more futuristic like they were built from an alien
world. Let's do some references pass to explore what that could look like." Six directions, one prompt each, for the
author to choose from; nothing here is in the game. Each is a temple the makers did not build the way they built the
stone houses already picked (`references/temples/`): built by hands from another world, or grown, or sung, or poured.
Each still reads as Hiraeth: the Moebius look, the big sky or the big hollow, the cloaked traveller with the round
jade fluid backpack dwarfed by the room, and a puzzle or a gadget at the room's middle (issue #336: much bigger rooms,
higher ceilings, the puzzle centred in the room, not through a door; the grappling hook and the gun's modes used in
the room itself).

Plain text prompts for the reference lab (`reference-lab.html`, `scripts/gen-reference.mjs --from
docs/design/alien-temple-prompts.md#<id>`), Gemini and OpenAI, no image references. A pick goes to the folder under
its direction. (The temples' references live in `references/temples/`, lower case, so these go to
`references/temples/alien/<id>/`.)

What every prompt keeps: the house style line (fine pen contours, flat pastel gouache, cream paper grain), the
traveller (an adult of ordinary height, never "small"), the makers' glyph (three dots over an arc that bows upward,
never a smile) somewhere in the alien work, as if the makers took the place over or learnt from it, and no lettering.

| Direction | Idea | Gadget / puzzle at its middle |
|---|---|---|
| `grown-hall` | a temple grown, not built: ribbed biotech halls of shell and membrane | bloom mode opening a sphincter-door bud at the hall's centre |

| `crystal-lattice` | a chamber of crystal lattice that bends light | turning prisms to carry one beam across the room to a lens |
| `mirror-metal` | rooms of fluid metal, a mirror floor that holds still and ripples | tether mode dragging a quicksilver sphere to a cradle |
| `resonant-cavern` | a cavern shaped by sound: ribbed horns and standing waves | the echo shell's note setting floating rings in line |
| `machine-guts` | inside a megastructure: the slow machine of a builder from elsewhere | weights hooked onto a great balance at the room's heart |

## The first run, read (2026-10-14)

One batch per direction, GPT Image 2 and Gemini, one candidate each, all in the lab (`reference-lab.html`, batches
`2026-10-14-19-52-02-*`). None picked: the author hearts them. What came out, strongest first:

- **`resonant-cavern`**: the strongest pair. Both read at once as alien (a cavern carved by sound, the horn walls and
  organ pipes) and as a temple puzzle at the middle of a big room (five rings over a basin, the shell's note through
  them). GPT's is the richer drawing, with the sky, two moons and ribbed horns; Gemini's has the flatter colour and
  bigger frame. Closest to the Moebius desert temples, so the easiest to carry into the game's look.
- **`grown-hall`**: GPT's is the most beautiful of the run: shell ribs, amber membrane panes, the bud as the
  centrepiece, a way out onto a pale sky, the most Moebius in line and colour. Gemini's is the same idea, softer and
  cuter. A clear "not built by the makers" look that still sits beside the stone temples.
- **`floating-stone`**: GPT's is a strong big-sky shot (the hook swing, the ring round the core, a cloud sea, real
  scale); Gemini's is a bolder comic panel with the hook line drawn well. The most futuristic of the six, the least
  like a room: an exterior or a guardian's arena more than a puzzle hall.
- **`crystal-lattice`**: both clear as a puzzle (three prisms, the beam, the lens on a dais), GPT's the more detailed
  hall with rainbow light on the floor. Reads more as sci-fi architecture than as Moebius; the lattice is busy for
  the ink pass at a distance.
- **`mirror-metal`**: GPT's is lovely and very Moebius (the dripping silver arches, the quicksilver floor and its
  reflection, the violet tether); Gemini's emptier and paler. A beautiful room, but the hardest to render in the game
  (a mirror floor).
- **`machine-guts`**: both readable (the balance, the hooked weight), GPT's the fuller interior; but it reads as a
  steampunk machine, close to the Engine-House and the First Garage we already have, so the least "alien".

Across the run: both providers drew the makers' glyph as a face (a frown or a smile under three dots) in most
pictures, against the prompt's "arc that bows upward"; ignore that in a pick, or say "never a face" in a rerun. GPT
Image 2 kept the house style better in every pair; Gemini framed most as a bordered comic panel.
