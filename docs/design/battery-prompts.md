# Where the makers' battery goes: reference prompts

2026-10-30, issue #799. The makers' battery (`docs/systems/traveller-kit.md`; then `src/battery-hang.js`, removed when the bracer was picked) hangs low on his
left hip since #745, an upright canister 23 cm tall and 14 cm across its rails. The author: "the battery hanging is
pretty bad, maybe we do reference explorations to find the right spot for it? maybe it should be horizontal and
slimmer?" These prompts try eight places for it, each drawing a slimmer battery than today's. The battery's look is the
current one (`references/Core Objects/Makers Battery/selected-2026-10-28/`): turquoise glass, glowing lavender-violet
fluid, brass caps and rims, two thin brass rails. Plain text prompts for the reference lab
(`node scripts/gen-reference.mjs --from docs/design/battery-prompts.md#<id>`), no image references.

Target folder: `references/characters/battery/<id>/`

What every prompt keeps: the traveller as in the game (a slim young adult, messy short black curly hair, an open
oversized salmon-coral overshirt over a white top, loose cream trousers rolled at the ankle, light sand suede ankle
boots, a short sword in its sheath slung across his back), three views on one sheet (back, back three-quarter, and a
close-up of the battery alone), the house style, a plain cream background, no hose or cable to his hand.
