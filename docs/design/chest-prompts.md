# The makers' chests, redesigned: reference prompts

2026-10-10. The author's list: "Redesign chests, they are boring, using new references." The chests are the
makers' boxes (docs/systems/boxes.md, `src/boxes/`): a seamless rounded shell with a pale four-point star on its
top, a compass on each side and a thin ray of light crossing it, hip high (`references/Core Objects/Makers Chest -
Seamless v3/`). It reads clean but plain, and it is the same box in every world. These prompts look for a chest with
more presence and a story: still the makers' (the same old, quiet people who left the temples and the glass
backpack), still opened the same way (it floats up, rocks three times and comes apart in light: the opening in
boxes.md), but with a **round jade glow at its heart**, the same living jade fluid as the traveller's round glass
backpack (`references/Core Objects/Round Backpack/`), so a chest and the backpack read as made by the same hands. A
world's chest wears that world's materials round the same core. Plain text prompts for the reference lab
(`reference-lab.html`, `scripts/gen-reference.mjs --from docs/design/chest-prompts.md#<id>`); the author picks.

Target folder: `references/Core Objects/Chests/<id>/`

What every prompt keeps: the house style, a hip-high chest (its top at an adult's hip, about 0.9 m, a little wider
than tall), the round jade glow (a glass sphere or lens of slowly swirling jade-green fluid set into the chest,
glowing softly from within), the makers' four-point star somewhere on it, rounded friendly forms rather than a
pirate's trunk, a plain cream background for the sheets, and no lettering. A cloaked adult traveller of ordinary
height with the round jade backpack stands beside it for scale where the prompt says so.
