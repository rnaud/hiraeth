# The makers' temples, room by room: reference prompts

2026-10-10. [temple-prompts.md](temple-prompts.md) made one reference for each temple's most characteristic hall and
one for its entrance; the author's picks are in `references/temples/<temple>/`. These are three more rooms of each
temple, chosen from its plan (docs/systems/temples.md, the room list at the top of each `src/temples/<world>.js`,
[../audits/temple-design-v1.27.md](../audits/temple-design-v1.27.md)): the room where the temple's one idea is taught
before the gadget, the chest room (the makers' chest on its dais and its try), and the room where the idea is turned
or carried on (the twist, or the room on past the chest). Each room is its own entry, so each gets its own folder.
Plain text prompts for the reference lab (`reference-lab.html`, `scripts/gen-reference.mjs --from
docs/design/temple-interior-prompts.md#<id>`); a pick goes to the folder below.

Target folder: `references/temples/rooms/<id>/`

What every prompt keeps, as in the halls already picked: the same materials, bands and palette as the temple's picked
hall, so a room reads as the same house; the makers' glyph (three dots over an arc that bows upward, carved, never a
smile) in friezes, or the temple's own carving where it has one (the Hush's three drops over a shut mouth, the
Lamp-House's three lamps over a hull); the house style; the cloaked traveller with the round jade fluid backpack (an
adult of ordinary height, never "small", dwarfed by the room); no lettering. The makers' chest is the same in every
temple: a low round chest of the temple's stone on a dais, its lid carved with the glyph, lit from above.
