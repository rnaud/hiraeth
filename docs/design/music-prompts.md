# Hiraeth world and temple music prompts

Draft replacement soundtrack, 16 October 2026. Each location has a recognisable melody, rhythmic identity and instrumental silhouette. Melancholy belongs especially to Home, the Lantern, the Eclipse and the lonely stretches of Vael; play, menace, bustle and exhilaration belong elsewhere. The reference to Ocarina of Time concerns variety and memorable place themes, not borrowed melodies or arrangements.

## Generation and listening workflow

Generate one world at a time, including each of its temples, and wait for the author's listening feedback before proceeding. Start with the Desert and the Rose House. Keep existing game recordings and their provenance unchanged until replacements are selected. These are proposed prompts, not descriptions of the currently installed audio.

Use Suno Songs, Advanced, instrumental (empty lyrics). Paste each paragraph into Styles. Aim for roughly two-minute exploration cues; duration and musical instructions are targets, not guaranteed controls. Ask for recurring sections and a return to the opening texture, then edit and audition the actual loop after selection. All tunes are original. Keep melodic identity audible at gameplay volume. Temples get independent melodies, increased scale and stronger tension, not merely louder overworld arrangements. Their exploration cues must tolerate repetition; separate boss cues can follow later.

The route has twelve worlds and fourteen temples. Vael includes the Sky Stones and Lorn includes the Deep Wood; preserve their regional contrast and both temples. The Clock-House is in the Glass Dunes. The Desert temple's current in-game name is the Rose House (older documents call it the Givers' House). Side worlds, the train, Home and the Lantern have no corresponding temple in the current temple registry; do not invent one. Retired locations are included at the end for completeness, but are not in the generation queue.
