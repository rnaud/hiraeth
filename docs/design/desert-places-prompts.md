# The Desert's key places: reference prompts

2026-10-09. The author asked for references of the Desert's key story places (the story is in
[../story-bible.md](../story-bible.md), "The Desert — The Tree That Drinks"): the giant's skull beyond the back
gate, the cave in the giant's chest under it, the burning tree of Qanat, and the giant ribcage. Plain text
prompts for the reference lab (`reference-lab.html`, `scripts/gen-reference.mjs --from
docs/design/desert-places-prompts.md#<id>`); a pick goes to the folder below.

Target folder: `references/The Desert/places/<id>/`

Every prompt keeps the house style of the other reference prompts and the Desert's palette (golden dunes, rose
canyon rock, turquoise water, lavender shadows), shows the small cloaked traveller with the round jade backpack for
scale, and asks for a wide establishing view a player would see.

### 1. The giant's skull (`skull`)

**Main: the fallen giant's skull beyond the back gate.**

```

### 2. The cave in the giant's chest (`skull-cave`)

**Main: the cave of shifting water, the pool dry.**

```
Environment concept art for a science fiction exploration game: a vast cave inside the chest of a fallen giant, reached through its skull's mouth: great curved ribs of bleached bone form the vaulted walls and ceiling like a cathedral, sand and rock filling the spaces between them, shafts of light falling through cracks in the bone. In the middle a round stone pool that should hold water lies dry, damp dark stains and a pale tide line on its sides; the pale roots of a giant tree hang down from the ceiling into the empty pool like curtains. Along the floor a carved channel runs from a crack in the far wall toward the pool, blocked by one huge fallen bone lying across it, a trickle of turquoise water glinting behind the bone. Faint wavering reflections of water play over the ribs even though the pool is dry. A small cloaked human traveller with a round jade fluid backpack stands at the pool's edge for scale.
