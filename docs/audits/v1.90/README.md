## What the evidence does not establish

No outside-player study, audible judgement, physical controller comfort, verified physical touch gesture, cold-install measurement or thermal soak is claimed. Xbox and Steam Deck hardware qualification are excluded at the author’s request. Scripted outcomes and screenshots are evidence about the stated scenarios, not proof of a newcomer’s understanding or success.

The new caravan and lowered landing stance are outside this frozen audit. Their separate release record identifies the shipped source and asset; the older Retroid measurements must not be used to qualify that new model. Individual reports preserve failed test setups, corrected captures and remaining coverage limits rather than treating every attempted check as a pass.
