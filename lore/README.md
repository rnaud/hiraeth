# Hiraeth: writing room

This folder accompanies the text-only dialogue pass of 2026-10-05. Start with
[the voice guide](voice-guide.md), then the relevant character sheet. The aim is
an inviting adventure through an impossible universe: understandable needs,
surprising people, immense things that are never entirely explained.

- [Traveller visual direction](characters/traveller-design.md): reference sheets, silhouette, clothing, and equipment.
- [The family](characters/family.md): the traveller, parents, Ilen, Lou, Tove, Moustache, and the ship.
- [Qanat and the desert](characters/desert.md): the opening cast and their relationships.
- [The other worlds](characters/worlds.md): histories, motives, and voice distinctions throughout the route.
- [Temple guides](characters/temple-guides.md): the people outside the eleven makers’ houses.
- [Plot review and quest proposals](plot-review.md): recommendations, not new quest requirements.
- [Continuity and scope](continuity.md): facts to preserve, documentation drift, and the files this pass covers.

`../LORE.md` remains the detailed record of the existing story and earlier design
decisions. Its quoted dialogue predates this pass; the shipped strings in
`src/story/`, `src/temples/`, `src/levels/`, and `src/items.js` are the current
wording. Do not copy an old quotation back over revised dialogue merely to make
it match that document.

In the character sheets, **established** means supported by the existing game or
LORE.md. **Writing premise** is additional interior history for consistent
performance. It is not a new event, quest, family relationship, or supernatural
answer the game now claims happened. Plot proposals are explicitly separate.

Simple choices such as “Thank you,” short useful directions, strong recurring
lines, proper names, and the makers’ glyph have been retained where they already
work. This is an editorial pass across the conversation corpus, not a requirement
to replace every word. No dialogue branches, flags, rewards, quest stages, or
world layouts have been added or removed. Keep this work in its worktree until
it is ready to integrate with the other ongoing work.
