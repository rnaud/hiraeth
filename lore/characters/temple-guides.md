# The people outside the makers' houses

These are neighbours with reasons to be here, not eleven interchangeable
oracles. Their established jobs anchor the voice; the interior premise below
is an editorial aid. The puzzles, gifts, conditions, and rewards are unchanged.
Each guide must distinguish what they have personally tried from what they
believe about the makers. On a repeat visit, put the next action first.

| Guide / house | Established connection | Writing premise and voice |
|---|---|---|
| Sabri / Givers' House, Qanat | Digs wells. | A person who trusts a measured depth more than an ancient claim. Friendly, physical instructions; mild professional envy of the makers' plumbing. |
| Vell / Warden's Well, City-Shaft | Lights the rim's lamps. | Notices which useful things the wealthy have forgotten are being maintained. Dry, economical, practical about levels and access. |
| Lark / Aerie, Vael | Sweeps the steps. | Has learned the bird's habits through daily work. Sparse speech, patient gestures; more verbal than Oïa, never a lore lecturer. |
| Ysel / Founders' Belfry, Vael II | Keeps the founders' bridge. | Respects a ritual most when it gets someone safely across. Orderly, ceremonious, with small admissions of inconvenience. |
| Wim / First Garage, Hangar | Winds the clocks. | Being on time matters even when gravity is undecided. Fussy affection for mechanisms; jokes about maintenance, not mystical destiny. |
| Brann / Engine-House, Buried Machine | Greases the door. | A great machine is a collection of jobs someone must do. Blunt, reassuring competence; explain the part and the action. |
| Sorrel / Builders' Greenhouse, Viridel | Sows its beds. | Patient with seedlings, less patient with visitors who mistake old stone for dead stone. Gentle specificity; knows what needs watering. |
| Tessa / Footprint, Garden of Spheres | Walks the rim. | Has noticed changes by returning to the same route. Curious, spatial, quietly playful; look from here, then compare. |
| Teasel / Hush-House, Lorn | Cuts reeds. | Has a worker's reasons to want the snappers calm. Dry swamp humour; unromantic about teeth. The cold gift affects the flowers/snappers, separate from the eggs' hatching quest. |
| Tamsy / Lamp-House, Lorn II | Counts the lamps. | Counting is a way of making sure nobody disappears into an unlit patch. Bright, attentive, explicit about missing lights. |
| Pell / Undertower, Signal Market | Listens at old stones. | The market's oldest things deserve to finish a sentence. Spare, wry, able to distinguish noise from a reply. This is not Qanat's potter Pell. |

Do not promise that finishing a house will explain the Singer or reunite an
absent family. Give the gift a local use and leave its deeper origin open.
The guides can be mistaken about meaning, but their puzzle instructions must
remain reliable. Their completion dialogue should notice the changed place,
not just congratulate the player for obtaining an upgrade.
