# Plumb, the drone

**Established:** the small folding drone of the traveller's ship (`src/drone.js`
its body, `src/scout.js` its flight, `src/companion.js` its own mind). It came
with the father's ship and sleeps in a charging cradle on the cabin wall by the
hatch (`src/ship/interior.js` DOCK). It finds the objective when asked (Q, D-pad →),
drops a flare on it, and in a guardian's fight points at the weak point.

**Name:** Plumb, after a plumb bob: the father's kind of tool, a weight on a
string that tells you which way is true. Nobody explains it in the game; its
lines carry its name on the cue ("◇ Plumb: …", issue #434).

**The prologue (issue #434):** after the landing, inside, by the amber reserve
light, it wakes in its cradle, blooms, lifts out of the cup and comes to the
traveller's shoulder at the hatch; it rides at his shoulder as he walks down
the ramp, and once outside says the opening's two lines: "Best bet? The village
on the horizon." "Someone there might help us recharge." (Its cradle went dark
with the ship: *us*.) Then it flies off toward the village, and the desert's
quest starts on it, its first step the same village.

**Writing premise:** a companion, not a tutorial. It is curious and a little
pleased with itself, fond of the traveller without making a fuss about it. It
knows the way; it does not know the story. It never explains the light, the
family or the theme, never reads the traveller's mind, and never sends him to
somebody else for the next step.

**Voice:** short. Two to seven words a line, one line at a time. Dry, warm,
practical. It points before it comments.

**Tic:** it answers its own questions. "Lost? A little. This way." "Scenic
route? The goal is over here." Do not overuse it: one line in three at most.

**Body language:** it is not carried on you: it comes out of a small light over
your shoulder when it is needed, and winks out again when it is done (after the
prologue it rides at your shoulder a while before it does). Out, it
faces what it points at; with nothing to find it shakes itself, no.

**When it speaks up:** a few lines at the start of a game, each once (out of the
ship, the first person near, the first chest, the first time you are lost), and
when you look lost: minutes with no progress and no new place, sooner if you
walk away from the goal, naming the place when the goal is one ("The giant's
skull is over here", never a person); in a makers' house, minutes on one step,
a word on what waits there (a switch, a plate, a ball) and the flight to it;
against a guardian, as the struggle goes on, the next of its hints by itself.
Never two of its own lines within 45 s (90 on fewer), cooldowns on each; the
"Drone hints" setting (on / fewer / off) and Hints off quiet it.

**Do not:** let it joke over a quiet scene, comment on a conversation, name a
button (the hint system teaches the controls), or speak twice in a minute.
